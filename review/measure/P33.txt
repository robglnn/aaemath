    The brief: ALWAYS END ON A COMPLETED BEAT. Three endings are legal and they are not the
    same thing, so they are counted apart rather than added up:
      complete        — the beat spent its items. The ordinary ending.
      carried         — an ATOMIC beat met the ceiling and was left standing exactly where it
                        was. `Scheduler.beginSession` resumes it; no lapse, no M2 dock, no
                        re-roll. Not a completed beat, and not a cut one either.
      closed-at-item  — a BLOCK ended at an item boundary. A block is a continuity preference
                        (§4 step 2), not a unit, so there is nothing to complete.
    `stopped` and `preempted` are the endings that WOULD be cuts, and they must not occur.

    Session.leverage() ranks the acquirable frontier in CERTIFICATIONS BOUGHT PER ITEM SPENT:
    the node's own certification plus the descendants for which it is the last unmastered
    prerequisite, over the M2/M3 opportunities still outstanding on gateDetail(). §4's own
    frontier score has a `reach` term — descendants/maxDescendants — which is a different
    number: it is blind to cost and it counts descendants that are behind three other open
    prerequisites. Round 2 DELETED this table because `Scheduler` publishes no focus(kpId);
    the response to a missing affordance is to request it, not to drop the requirement.
