C12 LEVERAGE — 'time spent where leverage on mastery is highest', and who decides it
    Session.leverage() ranks the acquirable frontier in CERTIFICATIONS BOUGHT PER ITEM SPENT:
    the node's own certification plus the descendants for which it is the last unmastered
    prerequisite, over the M2/M3 opportunities still outstanding on gateDetail(). §4's own
    frontier score has a `reach` term — descendants/maxDescendants — which is a different
    number: it is blind to cost and it counts descendants that are behind three other open
    prerequisites. Round 2 DELETED this table because `Scheduler` publishes no focus(kpId);
    the response to a missing affordance is to request it, not to drop the requirement.

    So the request goes out as learn:session {phase:'focus'} and this is what became of it.
