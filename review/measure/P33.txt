C11 DOES THE FORECAST DESCRIBE WHAT THE ENGINE SERVES?
    `candidates()` is a model of §4's own order: the due-time queue, then the frontier score
    with the same five terms and the same two-open cap. Round 1 ordered it by a leverage table
    of this layer's own invention that the engine had no way to hear, and — worse — ENDED over
    half of all sittings on it. The table is gone and nothing ends a sitting on a forecast now.

    Two horizons, and only one of them is a real test.
      NEXT BEAT  — at every beat boundary the live plan names what should come next, and the
                   engine is then asked. This is falsifiable and it is the number to read.
      WHOLE SITTING — the multiset of every beat forecast at the door against every beat
                   served. A sprinter's sitting is thirty beats and each acquisition block
                   moves the frontier score of the node it ran on, so the identity of beat 24
                   is not knowable at beat 1. Reported anyway, because deleting an
                   unflattering number is how a claim rots — but it never decided anything.
      SIZE       — planned minutes at the door against actual. The size IS what this layer
                   decides, so this is the forecast that has to be good.
